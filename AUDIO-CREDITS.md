## Booth announcer clips (`public/audio/announcers/`)

Generated by `npm run bake-audio` (`scripts/bake-audio.mjs`) from `src/audio/announcer-lines.json`.

- Engine: [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts) (LGPL-3.0 / GPL-3.0 tool) calling Microsoft Edge neural text-to-speech voices. Used offline at bake time only; the app ships and plays the resulting MP3 files and never calls a TTS service for the booth.
- Big Red Brannigan: `en-US-GuyNeural` (rate +6%, pitch -6Hz).
- Smooth Eddie Breeze: `en-US-BrianNeural` (rate -8%, pitch -2Hz).
- Characters and lines are original; no real announcer or celebrity voice is imitated.
- Voice output is subject to Microsoft's terms for its speech services. For a public/commercial release, consider re-baking with a voice whose license explicitly covers redistribution (the script takes a voice config at the top).
- Placeholders are baked as fixed stand-ins: `{word}` → "that spelling", `{runs}` → "2", `{inning}` → "3".
